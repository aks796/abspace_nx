Angry Birds Orbital Escapade's worlds
=====================================

Orbital Escapade is ShadowBird81's mod of the PC edition of Angry Birds Space.
The port can add its two new worlds to the game as two more planets on the
episode screen, after Danger Zone: Vege-toids and Omelettification (29 levels
and a boss each), with the prototype birds they are played with -- the Iron,
Pink, Boomerang, Black Hole and Drill birds -- in those two worlds only.

Download the mod from https://gamejolt.com/games/OrbitalEscapade/1016788,
extract it on a computer, and copy its "data" folder (the one next to
AngryBirdsOrbitalEscapade.exe) here. Copying the whole extracted folder into
/switch/abspace_nx works too: the game finds the mod's data folder by what is
in it (its scripts and the levels/theme1 and theme2 folders), whatever the
folders are called.

Nothing is converted or copied: the game reads the mod where it is. Without
the mod the game is unchanged. The port ships none of the mod.

The mod is the whole PC game (137 MB); the two worlds use about 22 MB of it.
After the first start with it (a moment at the main menu), the port removes
the rest of it from this folder: pictures, sounds and level folders the two
worlds do not use. Keep your own copy of the mod elsewhere if you want it
whole.

Nothing of the game itself changes either: its worlds, main menu, banner and
loading screens stay as they are, and the mod's changes to the game's own
birds and blocks (Big Red's shockwave when it lands; the Black and Ice
birds' power still usable for 2.5 s after a hit; weaker gravity fields,
bigger portals) only apply in the mod's two worlds.

The powers: the Iron bird shoots an egg where you tap; the Pink bird's beam
lifts what it hits (tap again to drop it); the Boomerang bird swings round
(tap again to send it back); the Black Hole bird opens a black hole (tap
again to come out); the Drill bird stops, bores in and explodes. With the
controller, A taps the middle of the screen, where the camera keeps the
flying bird: the Iron and Pink birds aim straight ahead. The touchscreen or
the hand cursor (R) aims them anywhere.

debug.log lines starting with [orbital] say what was added and any error.
